Product

One platform. Two apps. No waiting room.

For doctors and admins

A web app for schedules, the clinic calendar and a questionnaire builder. Only what you need on screen — the rest one click away.

For patients

A mobile app to browse clinics, book an appointment and fill in the pre-visit questionnaire before the doctor joins.

Consults that write themselves

HD video calls with live transcription, and AI that drafts the visit note for the doctor to review and sign.
